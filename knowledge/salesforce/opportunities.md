# BON AI - Salesforce Opportunities Knowledge

## What is an Opportunity?

An Opportunity represents a sales deal with a customer.

Every Opportunity belongs to one Account.

One Opportunity can contain multiple Products.

--------------------------------------------------

# OPPORTUNITY INFORMATION

## Opportunity Name

Name of the sales deal.

--------------------------------------------------

## Account Name

Customer linked with this Opportunity.

--------------------------------------------------

## Close Date

Expected or actual closing date.

--------------------------------------------------

## Stage

Current sales stage.

Stages may include:

- Payment Received
- Confirmation from Account
- Billing
- Closed

--------------------------------------------------

## Type

Opportunity type.

--------------------------------------------------

## Probability (%)

Winning probability.

--------------------------------------------------

## Annual Revenue

Estimated customer revenue.

--------------------------------------------------

## Forecast Category

Forecast category.

Example:

Commit

--------------------------------------------------

## Lead Source

Source from which opportunity originated.

--------------------------------------------------

## Closed Lost Reason

Reason if opportunity is lost.

--------------------------------------------------

## Deal Approval Status

Approval status of the deal.

--------------------------------------------------

## Total Amount

Total opportunity value.

--------------------------------------------------

## Monthly Target

Monthly sales target.

--------------------------------------------------

## Opportunity Currency

Currency used.

Example:

INR
