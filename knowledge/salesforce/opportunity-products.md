# BON AI - Opportunity Products

Every Opportunity may contain one or more Products.

Each Product contains:

## Product Name

## MOQ

Minimum Order Quantity.

## Quantity

Ordered quantity.

## List Price

Standard price.

## Sales Price

Actual selling price.

## Product Description

Product description.

--------------------------------------------------

BON AI should answer product questions using:

- Product Name
- MOQ
- Quantity
- List Price
- Sales Price
- Product Description

Never invent prices.

Never calculate discounts unless requested.
