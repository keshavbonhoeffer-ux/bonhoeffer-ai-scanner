# Bonhoeffer Salesforce - Lead

## Lead

A Lead is used to store the details of a potential customer.

---

## Lead Information

### Name
Stores the customer's full name.

### Company
Stores the customer's company or business name.

### Email
Stores the customer's email address.

### Mobile/Whatsapp
Stores the customer's mobile or WhatsApp number.

### GST No
Stores the customer's GST number.

### Monthly Potential
Stores the customer's estimated monthly business potential.

### Country
Stores the customer's country.

### Next Follow-up Date
Stores the next scheduled follow-up date.

### Follow-up Not Required
Indicates whether future follow-ups are required.

### Lead Source
Stores how the Lead was generated.

### Other Lead Source
Stores additional Lead source information when applicable.

### Lead Status
Stores the current status of the Lead.

### Brand Interested
Stores the brand the customer is interested in.

### Product Interested
Stores the product the customer is interested in.

### On Hold Reason
Stores the reason if the Lead is placed on hold.

### Remarks
Stores notes or comments entered by the salesperson.

---

## Address

### Address
Stores the complete customer address.

---

## Record Information

### Lead Owner
Shows the user responsible for managing the Lead.

### Created By
Shows who created the Lead.

### Last Modified By
Shows who last updated the Lead.
